# Vocabulary: Could we build Iron Man's arc reactor?

Hi everyone,

more than one meaning, so the meaning used in the video is marked with ★.

---

## Science words

**nuclear** (adjective)
1. ★ Connected with the centre (nucleus) of an atom and the energy it releases.
   *a nuclear reactor, nuclear power*
2. Connected with atomic weapons.
   *nuclear war*
3. **nuclear family**: parents and their children only.

**reactor** (noun)
1. ★ A large machine that produces energy from nuclear reactions.
   *We have already invented nuclear reactors.*
2. A container where a chemical reaction takes place.

**fusion** (noun)
1. ★ Joining the centres of two atoms to make one, which releases a huge
   amount of energy. (The opposite of *fission*, splitting atoms.)
   *a fusion reactor*
2. A mix of two different styles.
   *jazz fusion, fusion cuisine*

**arc** (noun)
1. A curved line, like part of a circle.
   *The ball flew in a high arc.*
2. ★ A bright flow of electricity jumping between two points.
   *an arc reactor*
3. **story arc**: how a story or character develops from start to end.

## Words with an unusual meaning here

**driver** (noun)
1. A person who drives a vehicle.
   *a taxi driver*
2. ★ Something that starts or pushes a process forward.
   *driver energy* = the energy you put in to start the reaction
   *Price is a key driver of sales.*
3. Software that lets a computer use a device.
   *a printer driver*

**shielding** (noun)
1. ★ Material that protects people from something dangerous, like radiation.
   *The reactor needs thick shielding.*
2. Protecting someone from harm or criticism.
   *She's always shielding her little brother.*

**space** (noun)
1. The area beyond Earth's atmosphere.
   *astronauts in space*
2. ★ An amount of room or area.
   *a very small space, a parking space*
3. A period of time.
   *in the space of a week*

**cooling** (noun / adjective)
1. ★ (noun) The process of making something colder.
   *cooling the reactor parts*
2. (adjective) Making you feel less hot.
   *a cooling drink*

**major** (adjective / noun)
1. ★ (adj.) Most important; main; serious.
   *the major challenge*
2. (noun) A university student's main subject.
   *Her major is biology.*
3. (noun) An army officer's rank.

**conditions** (noun, plural)
1. ★ The exact situation needed for something to happen.
   *fusion conditions*
2. The weather or the environment.
   *driving conditions, working conditions*
3. Rules in an agreement.
   *terms and conditions*

**fit** (verb)
1. ★ To be the right size or shape for a place.
   *It would never fit on a human body.*
2. To be the right size for someone (clothes).
   *These shoes don't fit.*
3. (adjective) Healthy and strong.
   *She runs every day and she's very fit.*

## Everyday words

**fictional** (adjective)
1. ★ Not real; invented for a story, film or game.
   *The arc reactor is fictional.*
   Compare: **fiction** (stories), **fact** (true information).

**energy / power** (nouns): similar but not the same
- **energy**: the ability to do work, which you put in or store.
  *large amounts of energy*
- **power**: energy being used, or the supply of electricity.
  *Iron Man uses an arc reactor for power.*
- Both can also describe people:
  *She's full of energy. He has a lot of power in the company.*

## Natural phrases to steal

**as much as I love…, (but)…**
Use it to say you like something before giving a "but".
*As much as I love pizza, I can't eat it every day.*

**on top of that**
= also, in addition (often adding another problem).
*The flight was late. On top of that, they lost my bag.*

**you wouldn't want (to)…**
A friendly way to warn someone.
*You wouldn't want to swim there — the water's freezing.*
